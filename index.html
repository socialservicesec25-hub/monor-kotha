Our story

A campus that listens is a campus that grows.

The origin of মনৰ কথা

The portal was officially initiated in 2026 by Deep Hazarika, Social Service Secretary, in collaboration with the Tinsukia College Student Union 2026-2027. It was established to bridge the communication gap between the student body and the college administration, replacing traditional paperwork with a modern, digital platform for transparent and immediate dialogue.

Every student deserves to be heard without administrative friction. By digitizing the feedback process, the portal ensures that no genuine concern is ignored or misplaced — and helps future student unions and college authorities quickly identify campus needs, resolve recurring issues, and continuously upgrade the college environment based on direct student input.
